# Flame positives (legacy path)

Controlled flames are **POSITIVE** for the fire class under the 2026-10-22 policy.
This folder stores CC-licensed candle/campfire/welding/fireworks/fireplace source
images formerly kept under hard_negatives/sources/wikimedia. They are used for
flame-positive validation/calibration and future training proposals; they are
not hard negatives. Per-image attribution: sources/wikimedia/attribution.jsonl.

Hard negatives remain in hard_negatives/sources/construction_dust and the B5
collection plan (docs/PHASE_B_PLAN.md).
